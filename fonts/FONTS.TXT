Fonts for VPA
==============

  To use one of these, copy it into your VPA directory, and put
  'Font=filename' into the [System] section of vpa.ini.

cp437.fnt
cp850.fnt
cp852.fnt
cp860.fnt
cp863.fnt
cp865.fnt
  Simple no-frills fonts for certain DOS codepages
latin1.fnt
  Simple no-frills font in Latin-1 encoding (Windows/Unix)
rusfont.fnt
  The standard cyrillic font built into VPA, codepage 866
sanserif.fnt
  Nice sans-serif font, codepage 437
thin.fnt
  A rather thin font, codepage 437
